Here are some of the photos from my trip to taipei from Janurary 2026!

The main reason I wanted to go to Taipei was to try out all the food, so you know as soon as we landed we headed to a night market (that night and almost every other night we were there).

These are the famous black pepper buns from the [Raohe Night Markets](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/516455044#map=17/25.050307/121.575677)!

We would also usually look for a new soy milk breakfast restaurant each day too! These photos are from a variety of places, but our favourite was probably this small "locals" restaurant that we stumbled across [天津豆漿](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/4031296868).

The classic thing to get at these breakfast places seemed to be savoury soy milk, shao bing you tiao, xio long bao and regular soy milk. 

![breakfast-1](breakfast-1.jpeg)
![breakfast-2](breakfast-2.jpeg)

If you don't know what "savoury soy milk" is, it probably sounds a but gross but its actually really tasty! Its fresh soy milk that has been poured over a small amount of vinegar, soy sauce and other things. The soy milk will split from the vinegar and give you clearer soup and milk solids that go well with the youtiao.

![breakfast-3](breakfast-3.jpeg)

This is called gao bao, It's quite well known overseas but I don't think I'd even call the times I've had this in australia the same dish. These have much jucier pork, fresh fluffy buns and a good balance with the pickles and seasoning. 

![guabao](guabao.jpeg)

I've been deep diving on fermentation books recently so you know I had to try the stinky tofu! It's super easy to find as it's sold at almost every night market. 

Like alot of fermented goodies, I end up getting a weird moorishness for them. It ended up being a daily tradition of trying the stinky tofu at each night market, eventually went down to the stinky tofu village too.
